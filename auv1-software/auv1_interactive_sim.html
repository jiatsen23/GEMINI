SLIDE 3 — 3-TIER GEMINI + GEMMA ONBOARD DISTILLATION

Gemini Pro/Flash Planning + Gemma Teacher → 20 Hz Student MLP

There is no internet underwater, and LLMs are too slow (500 ms+) to sit inside a 20 Hz PID loop. AUV1 solves this with a 3-Tier Gemini Hierarchy: (1) Gemini Pro / Flash (`nl_planner.py`) compiles natural language at the surface into Boustrophedon survey or gate trajectories; (2) Quantized Gemma 2B / Gemini Nano Teacher (`llm_distill.py`) runs offline on the Raspberry Pi 5 to interpret 360° Sonar SLAM scenes; and (3) Online DAgger Distillation trains a fast 10→16→3 Student MLP executing in <0.1 ms inside the 20 Hz flight controller.

3-TIER GEMINI HIERARCHY
• Tier 1 (Surface): Gemini Pro / Flash
• Tier 2 (Pi 5 Edge): Gemma 2B Teacher
• Tier 3 (20 Hz Loop): `10→16→3` Student MLP
• Safety: ±25° Pitch & 0.35m Floor Guard
